YouTube Mixer Instructions

🔍 Searching & Loading Videos

  1. Search Videos: Type in the search bar at the top and click "Search" (or press Enter).
    • A modal will open with video results
    • Hover over any video to see action buttons
    • Click "Load in Deck A" or "Load in Deck B" to instantly load the video
    • Press ESC to close the search modal
  2. Manual URL Entry: Alternatively, paste a YouTube video URL (e.g., https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=...) into the input field for Video 1 or Video 2, then click "Load".

🎮 Playback Controls

Control each video using buttons or keyboard shortcuts:

Video 1 (Deck A):
Q = Play
W = Pause
E = Stop
Video 2 (Deck B):
A = Play
S = Pause
D = Stop

🎚️ Audio Mixer (Crossfader)

Use the slider at the bottom to mix audio between the two videos:

  • Slide LEFT: Increases Video 1 volume, decreases Video 2 volume
  • Slide RIGHT: Increases Video 2 volume, decreases Video 1 volume
  • Center: Balanced volume (both videos at equal volume)

Fader Shortcuts:

  • ← / → (Arrow Keys): Fine adjustment (5% steps)
  • Shift + Mouse Scroll: Adjust fader with mouse wheel

💡 Pro Tips

  • Search for videos directly without leaving the mixer
  • Use keyboard shortcuts for hands-free DJ-style mixing
  • The crossfader uses constant power panning for smooth transitions
  • You can load videos while others are playing

Enjoy mixing your videos!
